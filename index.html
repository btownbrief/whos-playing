Ground rules

  1. Meet somewhere public the first time. Courts, the gym, the trailhead.
  2. Show up when you said you would. If you can't, say so — early.
  3. Be honest about your level. A mismatch wastes both your evenings.
  4. Nobody here is vetted by anyone. Trust your gut; report anything off.
  5. This board is for adults, 18 and up.
  6. Backcountry partners: confirm certs and gear in person before you skin.
  7. Only the person who posted a call sees the replies to it. Your contact info never appears on the board.
